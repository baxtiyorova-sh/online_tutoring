Frequently asked questions

How do I book a lesson?

Create a student profile, search for a tutor, and tap a green hour. It moves to My lessons straight away.

How do I offer lessons?

Choose Tutor in your profile, add your subjects and rate, then open hours under My schedule.

Where is my data kept?

Only in this browser. Clearing site data or using another device starts fresh.
